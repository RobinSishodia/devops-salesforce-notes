## The 4 Building Blocks

| Block | What it is | Key point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Files** | Modern and versioned | Up to 2 GB |
| **Attachments** | Legacy and basic | Up to 25 MB |
| **Libraries** | Shared team folders | Member roles |
| **Sharing** | Links to records | Viewer or Collab |

---

## Interview Q&A

### 1. Files or Attachments?

**Files are modern**: versions, sharing and preview.

> **Tip:** Migrate old attachments.

### 2. What are the size limits?

**Up to 2 GB per file.** Legacy attachments allow 25 MB.

> **Tip:** Check your org limits.

### 3. How are files shared?

Through **links to records**, people, groups or libraries.

> **Tip:** One file, many records.

### 4. Viewer or Collaborator?

**Viewer can view and download.** Collaborator can also edit.

> **Tip:** Default to Viewer.

### 5. What is a library?

A **shared folder** for files, with member roles and permissions.

> **Tip:** Use it for team content.

### 6. Uploading a new version?

The file **keeps its history** and the same links.

> **Tip:** No need to re-share.

## Scenario Question

**"Reps must attach signed contracts to Opportunities. How?"**

1. **Add** Files to the page layout
2. **Store** contracts in a library
3. **Set** members to Viewer
4. **Test** upload and preview

## Quick Cheat Sheet

- **Files** = modern
- **Attachments** = legacy
- **Limit** = 2 GB per file
- **Share** = record links
- **Viewer** = view only
- **Version** = keeps history

## Key Takeaways

- Use Files, not Attachments
- Share through records and libraries
- Give Viewer unless edit is needed
- Upload versions, do not duplicate
